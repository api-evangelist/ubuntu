Yazan Balawneh discovered that GStreamer Good Plugins incorrectly handled certain FLAC audio streams. An attacker could possibly use this issue to obtain sensitive information. (CVE-2026-17072) Seonwook Kim discovered that GStreamer Good Plugins incorrectly parsed certain AVI files.
