It was discovered that KissFFT incorrectly handled certain large Fourier transform sizes on 32-bit architectures. An attacker could possibly use this issue to cause KissFFT to crash, resulting in a denial of service, or execute arbitrary code. (CVE-2025-34297) It was discovered that KissFFT incorrectly handled certain multidimensional Fourier transform sizes.
