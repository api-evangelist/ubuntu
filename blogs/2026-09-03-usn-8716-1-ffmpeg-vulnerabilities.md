It was discovered that FFmpeg incorrectly handled certain crafted media files in the VobSub subtitle demuxer. An attacker could possibly use this issue to cause a denial of service or execute arbitrary code. (CVE-2026-64830) It was discovered that FFmpeg incorrectly handled certain crafted DTS audio streams in the S/PDIF muxer.
