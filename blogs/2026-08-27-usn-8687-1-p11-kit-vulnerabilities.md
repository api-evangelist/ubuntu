It was discovered that p11-kit incorrectly handled certain RPC messages. A local attacker could use this issue to cause p11-kit to crash, resulting in a denial of service. (CVE-2026-13757) It was discovered that p11-kit incorrectly handled nested attribute decoding on 32-bit systems.
